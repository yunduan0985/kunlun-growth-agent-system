1/ 商业创意生成器

“我有500美元和每周10小时的时间。请分析当前市场空白，以及我在[您的技能]方面的技能，并提出5个我本月可以启动的商业想法。请包括启动成本、时间表和首期收入预测。”

这个提示简直太强大了。

2/ THE COMPETITOR DESTROYER

"Act as a market analyst. Research [competitor name] and identify 3 weaknesses in their offering. Then create a differentiation strategy I could execute with limited budget to capture their customers."

Watch them panic.
2/ 竞争对手克星

“担任市场分析师。研究[竞争对手名称]并找出其产品或服务中的3个弱点。然后制定一个差异化战略，在预算有限的情况下，帮助我赢得他们的客户。”

看着他们惊慌失措。

3/ THE LANDING PAGE ARCHITECT

"Build a complete landing page copy for [your business idea]. Include hero headline, 3 benefit sections, social proof framework, FAQ, and 5 different CTA variations. Optimize for conversion psychology."

Copy. Paste. Launch.
3/ 着陆页架构师

“为[你的商业理念]撰写完整的着陆页文案。包括醒目的标题、3 个利益点、社会认同框架、常见问题解答和 5 个不同的行动号召（CTA）版本。优化转化心理学。”

复制。粘贴。启动。

4/ THE PRICING STRATEGIST

"Analyze pricing models in [your industry]. Calculate optimal price points based on value perception, competitor pricing, and target customer willingness to pay. Include 3 tier structures with psychological anchoring."

This prints money.
4/ 定价策略师

“分析[您所在行业]的定价模型。根据价值感知、竞争对手定价和目标客户的支付意愿，计算最佳价格点。包含具有心理锚定效应的三级结构。”

这相当于印钞。

5/ THE CONTENT MACHINE

"Create a 30-day content calendar for [your business]. Include post ideas, hooks, optimal posting times, and content types (carousel, thread, video). Focus on viral potential and audience growth."

Never run out of ideas again.
5/ 内容机器

为[您的企业]制定一份为期30天的内容日历。内容包括帖子创意、吸引眼球的亮点、最佳发布时间以及内容类型（轮播图、帖子串、视频）。重点关注病毒式传播潜力和受众增长。

从此不再为创意枯竭而烦恼。

6/ THE EMAIL SEQUENCE BUILDER

"Write a 7-email welcome sequence for [your product/service]. Include subject lines with 40%+ open rates, storytelling frameworks, and strategic CTAs. Map customer psychology journey from awareness to purchase."

Automated sales while you sleep.
6/ 电子邮件序列构建器

为[您的产品/服务]撰写一套包含7封欢迎邮件的系列邮件。邮件主题应包含打开率超过40%的内容、故事框架和策略性行动号召。绘制从认知到购买的客户心理历程图。

睡梦中也能自动销售。

7/ THE AD COPY ASSASSIN

"Generate 10 Facebook/Instagram ad variations for [your offer]. Include scroll-stopping hooks, benefit-driven body copy, and urgency-based CTAs. Optimize for $0.50 CPM and 5%+ CTR."

Print customers on demand.
7/ 广告文案刺客

为您的产品/服务生成 10 个 Facebook/Instagram 广告版本。广告内容应包含吸引眼球的开头、突出产品优势的文案以及营造紧迫感的行动号召。优化目标为每千次展示费用 0.50 美元，点击率 5% 以上。

按需打印。

8/ THE FUNNEL ARCHITECT

"Design a complete sales funnel for [your business]. Map awareness → consideration → conversion stages. Include lead magnets, tripwires, core offers, and upsells with expected conversion rates at each stage."

This is the blueprint.
8/ 漏斗架构师

“为[您的企业]设计一个完整的销售漏斗。绘制认知→考虑→转化阶段的流程图。包含引流产品、诱导性产品、核心产品和追加销售产品，并预测每个阶段的转化率。”

这是蓝图。

9/ THE OBJECTION CRUSHER

"List every possible objection for [your product/service]. For each objection, write 3 counter-arguments using social proof, logic, and emotional triggers. Format as FAQ section."

Handle doubts before they exist.
9/ 异议粉碎机

列出所有可能针对[您的产品/服务]的反对意见。针对每一种反对意见，运用社会认同、逻辑和情感诉求，写出3个反驳论点。格式为常见问题解答部分。

在疑虑出现之前就将其消除。

10/ THE PARTNERSHIP FINDER

"Identify 20 potential partnership opportunities in [your niche]. Include contact strategies, pitch templates, and mutual benefit propositions. Prioritize by ease of approach and potential impact."

Scale through others.
10/ 合作伙伴查找器

“在[你的细分领域]中确定20个潜在的合作机会。包括联系策略、推介模板和互惠互利方案。按接洽难易程度和潜在影响进行优先级排序。”

通过他人扩大规模。

11/ THE AUTOMATION ARCHITECT

"Map every repetitive task in [your business type]. For each task, suggest automation tools, implementation steps, and time savings. Calculate ROI on each automation."

Buy back your time.
11/ 自动化架构师

“梳理[你的业务类型]中的所有重复性任务。针对每个任务，提出自动化工具、实施步骤和节省时间的建议。计算每次自动化的投资回报率。”

把时间买回来。

12/ 客户头像创建器

比他们自己更了解他们。

Attention = money.

小小的调整就能带来巨大的收益。
